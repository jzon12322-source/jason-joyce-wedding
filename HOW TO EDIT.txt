JASON & JOYCE WEDDING INVITATION

TO EDIT (no coding)
 1. Keep this whole folder together: index.html, assets and fonts.
 2. Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome or Edge.
 3. Tap "Open invitation", then tap "Edit invitation" at the bottom left.
 4. Change any text, add or adjust photos, change the song, QR codes and more.
 5. Tap "Save changes". When asked where to save, pick this same index.html
    and replace it.
    (If your browser just downloads a file instead, move the new index.html
    from your Downloads folder into this folder and replace the old one.)

TO PUT IT ONLINE FOR GUESTS
 Upload this whole folder to a free website host such as Netlify Drop
 (app.netlify.com/drop). After every edit, upload the folder again.

WHO CAN EDIT?
 The "Edit invitation" button only appears when the file is opened from a
 computer, never on the online link. Guests can't change your website.
